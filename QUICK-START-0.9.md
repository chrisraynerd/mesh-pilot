# Mesh Terminal 0.9

Your Heltec's channels and contacts load after connection.

## Contacts

1. Tap **Contacts** on the channel screen.
2. Tap a name for details, then **Message** to open their conversation.
3. **Fave** puts them near the top of the list. **Edit** lets you rename or
   remove them. Keep the Heltec powered for at least five seconds after a contact change.

The top filter cycles through people, repeaters, rooms, sensors, favourites,
recently heard nodes and all types. **Find** searches names or the beginning
of a public key. To clear a search, delete the text and tap **Done**.

**Add** creates a contact using their name and complete 64-digit public key.
New discoveries show **New**; **Edit > Save** keeps one on the Heltec.

## Nodes and repeaters

Choose **Net > Nodes**. Tap a repeater for **Status** or **Find path**.
A saved name alone does not tell you whether it is reachable. A matching reply
confirms the return path. **Load** refreshes the Heltec's list.

## Add a named channel

1. Choose **Manage** at the top of the channel screen.
2. Select **+ Add channel** in an empty slot.
3. Tap **Name**, enter the exact `#name`, and tap **Done**.
4. Leave the type as **#channel**, tap **Save**, then **Confirm**.
5. Go back to the channel list to open it.

Use **123** on the name keyboard to find `#`. Names are case-sensitive.
Anyone who knows a hashtag channel's name can join it.

## Private channels

In the channel editor, enter a name and tap **New key** to create a private
channel, or switch to **shared key > Enter key** to join one with its existing
32-digit key. Other members need the same key. Keep private keys confidential.

An existing channel can also be edited or removed through **Manage**.
Replacing its key starts a fresh local chat view; earlier raw history is retained
in the journal. Channel saves are checked by reading them back from the Heltec.

**Net > Leave** disconnects from the radio. Your existing bonding, calibration
and message history remain saved.
